## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Architecture

Built upon Google Stitch UI's **Warm Editorial Atelier** design system:
- **Typography**: `Syne` (display headlines), `Instrument Serif` (editorial lead-ins and pagination), `Plus Jakarta Sans` (body and UI), and `JetBrains Mono` (code and tags).
- **Layout Rhythm**: Planar depth, 1px low-contrast borders, radial ambient glow aura (`glow-aura`), and softened squircle framing (`rounded-[2.5rem]`).
- **Strict Data-Presentation Decoupling**: Single source of truth in `src/data/portfolioData.ts`.
- **Theme Support**: Seamless transitions between Dark foundation (`#0B0F17`) and Light mode (`#FAF9F6`).
- **No-Slop Discipline**: Zero fake metrics, zero percentage skill bars, and zero fabricated claims.
