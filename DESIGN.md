## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the discipline, craft, and distinct perspective of a senior creative developer. It bridges contemporary Swiss-inspired functional clarity with tactile editorial print warmth. The visual posture avoids disposable digital trends in favor of timeless typographical command, confident negative space, and disciplined layout rhythm.

The target audience spans creative agencies, design-forward technology founders, and high-end cultural clients seeking bespoke digital execution. The interface feels intentional, curated, quiet, and authoritative—elevating code and interactive craft to fine art.

## Colors

The palette establishes a warm, tactile gallery mood. The canvas is dominated by `#F9F8F5` (warm archival cream), supported by crisp charcoal-to-black `#121212` for primary ink and typography. 

- **Primary (`#121212`):** Dense carbon black applied to major condensed headlines, primary actions, and prominent structural rules.
- **Secondary (`#6E6B65`):** Warm mineral gray reserved for metadata, dates, secondary descriptions, and subtle hairline dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Amber ochre used with extreme scarcity for status badges (e.g., "Available for Q3/Q4"), active states, or interactive highlights.
- **Neutral (`#F9F8F5`):** Cream paper foundation, complemented by elevated container surface tone `#F1EFEA` and hairline structural border tone `#E4E0D8`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between bold industrial modernism and delicate literary grace. 

- **Display & Section Titles:** Set in uppercase `Oswald` to deliver high-impact, condensed architectural structure.
- **Editorial Sub-statements & Cursive Counterpoints:** Cast in `Newsreader` in italic or light roman styles, introducing human warmth directly adjacent to condensed uppercase titles (e.g., paired "Hey, *there*" or editorial annotations).
- **Body & Functional UI:** Typeset in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean, neutral readability across long project case studies, client testimonial quotes, and metadata grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an asymmetrical 12-column fluid grid framed within a generous outer canvas margin. Wide negative space separates editorial chapters to let featured imagery and interactive prototypes breathe.

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid, `4rem` outer margin, `2.5rem` gutter. Major headlines span 6 to 8 columns with intentional offsets for editorial flow.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `2.5rem` margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Side-by-side elements collapse into 4-column blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `1.5rem` margin, `1rem` gutter. Text stacks vertically, and display typography scales proportionally.
- Vertical cadence enforces ample room: major sections use `space-xl` (or multiples) for theatrical pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows and heavy multi-tier elevations. Depth is defined through planar architectural framing:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Panels, project cells, and floating pill controls use crisp 1px borders tinted in `#E4E0D8`.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Secondary cards and media backdrops sit on `#F1EFEA`, creating a natural lift against the `#F9F8F5` page canvas without casting shadows.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** In the rare case of persistent top navigation or modal overlays, depth is achieved with a faint backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) tinted with 85% opacity of `#F9F8F5` and bordered by a 1px border.

## Shapes

The system pairs razor-sharp content framing with pill-shaped interactive anchors:

- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Status Badges, Chips):** Use full pill geometry (`roundedness: 3`, or `rounded-full`) to create a soft, ergonomic contrast against structured typography and rectilinear imagery.
- **Media & Project Containers:** Kept crisp or gently softened with a subtle radius (`0.5rem` to `1rem`) to maintain an authentic print-brochure framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Fully rounded pill container in `#121212` with `#F9F8F5` text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (14px, weight 600). Hover state subtly scales up to 102% or transitions to a dark umber tone.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Fully rounded pill with a 1px border of `#121212` or `#E4E0D8`, transparent surface, and carbon text.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped status tokens (e.g., "Available for work") feature a warm neutral background (`#F1EFEA`), a delicate amber indicator dot (`#D97706`), and uppercase or tracked micro-typography.

### Cards & Project Showcase
- Built with a 1px border in `#E4E0D8` and surface color `#F1EFEA`. 
- Media is presented edge-to-edge or with equal `1.5rem` interior matting, accompanied by uppercase condensed tags (`Oswald`) and italic serif captions (`Newsreader`).

### Input Fields
- Understated rectangular or pill inputs featuring a transparent fill, 1px `#E4E0D8` border, and active focus state shifting directly to `#121212` with zero offset glow.

### Lists & Experience Index
- Monospaced or tabular experience lists formatted as horizontal bands divided by clean 1px hairline horizontal rules (`#E4E0D8`), with years aligned left, roles in center, and technology stacks or companies right-aligned.
